Lab 3: Assess SmartPLS Regression Results

Goal

Assess regression output systematically and prepare a results assessment table.

Assessment Items

Record:

  • Dependent variable
  • Sample size
  • R-square
  • Adjusted R-square
  • Coefficients
  • Standardized coefficients
  • Standard errors
  • t-values
  • p-values
  • Confidence intervals
  • Diagnostic notes

Questions

  1. Which predictor has the largest standardized coefficient?
  2. Which predictors are statistically significant?
  3. Are any coefficient signs unexpected?
  4. Does R-square indicate weak, moderate, or strong explanatory power for your context?
  5. Does the QQ plot suggest serious residual non-normality?
  6. Should robust standard errors be reported?

Deliverable

Complete:

  • Regression Assessment Checklist
  • Export Tracking Sheet
