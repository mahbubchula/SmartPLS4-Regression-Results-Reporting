Assignment 1: Data Preparation and SmartPLS Import

Goal

Prepare and import a dataset into SmartPLS 4.

Tasks

  1. Import smartpls_regression_training_data.csv.
  2. Verify sample size.
  3. Complete a codebook for at least 10 variables.
  4. Identify dependent variable, predictors, controls, and indicators.
  5. Explain missing-data handling.

Deliverable

Submit a short report with dataset screenshot and completed codebook.
